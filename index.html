About us

An inclusive place to find your voice

The Green Road Secondary School Debate Society is an inclusive platform for students to participate in debating. Being the sister club to our school's English Club, our mission is to cultivate a culture of speaking up in school and in life by building students' confidence.

We welcome anyone who is interested in developing their critical thinking, public speaking and communication skills. We host weekly club sessions to teach students debating!

What you'll build with us

  • Critical thinking
  • Public speaking
  • Communication
  • Confidence, in school and in life

The Debate Team

Debate extends outside of our society

We have a team of people we specially train to represent our school in competitive debating tournaments. Here is our main squad.

For us, debating is a passion that we hold dear to our hearts. We find opting into discourse meaningful, as we have seen how our words can impact those around us, and the importance of building a community that is accepting of each other's views. We believe that debate has transformed our lives and how we see the world around us. We believe it can change yours too.

Why should you try debating?

Skills that travel with you

Speak up with confidence

We offer a safe and welcoming environment for students to voice their thoughts without being judged.

Sharpen your thinking

Improve your critical thinking skills. Learn how to logically build arguments and analyze points.

Use it every day

Most importantly, these soft skills help you in your everyday life too. Whether it's just a class presentation or choosing your future career, debate can shape how you see the world in unfathomable ways.
